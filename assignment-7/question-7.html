A short essay on attention

The generous pause

Good typography does not demand attention. It gives attention back.

There is a particular pleasure in a page that knows how to make room. Its words are not crowded by their neighbors, and its ideas arrive with enough air around them to be understood.

Clarity is a form of care. It appears in a comfortable line-height, a measured letter-spacing, and a sentence allowed to finish its thought. Reading becomes a quieter kind of conversation.

Set with intention.
