One width, two outcomes

Padding changes the total size depending on the box-sizing model.

content-boxDeclared width: 260pxPadding and border are added outside.

Default sizing makes the rendered box wider than 260px.

border-boxDeclared width: 260pxPadding and border fit inside.

Border-box keeps the rendered width at exactly 260px.

Both boxes share the same width, padding, and border.
