A note on scale

Good type
finds its size.

A heading can feel substantial on a wide screen and remain calm on a small one. Relative units and clamp() let type respond without a pile of arbitrary overrides.

01

Give every line enough room to breathe. The measure, line height, and space around the text matter just as much as the font size.

Scale with intention. Read at every size.
